## Featured project

### 🎓 CodevolveX

A learning platform where instructors build and publish video courses, and students buy, watch and track them — with real payments and instructor analytics.

- **Courses & learning flow:** Searchable catalogue, course detail pages, cart + Razorpay checkout, video player with per-lecture progress, ratings & reviews, and PDF receipts. Instructors get a multi-step course builder + Recharts analytics dashboard.
- **Role-based access:** Separate student and instructor experiences, with an admin course-review workflow (approve/reject) in progress.
- **Auth & security:** JWT authentication, server-verified Razorpay payments, and transactional email via Brevo.
